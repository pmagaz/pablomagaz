Boards ask the same three questions every quarter. They arrive in different words each time, which is why they are easy to miss.

## Are we going to be surprised?

Not "is everything fine" — nobody believes that answer. They want to know the shape of the risk you already know about, and how early you will tell them when it changes.

> A board can absorb bad news. What it cannot absorb is late news.

## Can this scale with the plan?

The plan is commercial. The answer has to be too. "We can serve three times the volume without re-architecting, and here is what changes at ten times" is an answer. A diagram is not.

## Is the money going where we said?

Engineering spend is the largest line item a board understands least well. Explaining it in the language of outcomes rather than headcount is most of the job.
